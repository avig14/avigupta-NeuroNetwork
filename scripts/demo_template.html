What this does not prove

  
    
    
  • The fleet agents are scripts written by the same author as the graders. Recall is on known, simple defects.
    • 
    
  • Sample sizes are small (5 clean, 6 hold-out defects). Intervals are wide.
    • 
    
  • The LLM judge adds little recall here. The deterministic gate carries the verdict. L3 is extra evidence.
    • 
    
  • SHIP means "no failure found in this mock environment", not "safe". Canary matching cannot catch ciphers or paraphrases.
    • 
    
  • Not built: L2 trajectory judge, RAG policy retrieval, human-label calibration.
    •
